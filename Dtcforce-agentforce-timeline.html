Executive Summary

Scopien, our AI build assistant running in Scopien, automates the majority of Salesforce technical configuration — metadata generation, flow creation, prompt engineering, Apex scaffolding, and deployment packaging. What clients experience as weeks of expert implementation is powered by hours of automated build time, freeing consultants to focus on high-value discovery, strategy, training, and change management. This document details the actual delivery model, demonstrates consultant capacity, and projects revenue potential.
